## The two tracks

| Track | Best for | Datasets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PETS2 + Jana2020** | Small-molecule and inorganic crystals | Tyrosine (cRED) and Slotaite (PED), from the [Jana cookbook](https://jana.fzu.cz/#cookbook-section), sections 13.10 and 13.11 |
| **DIALS + Phenix** | Macromolecular compounds | Zenodo datasets, listed on the [software page](../software.md#dials-datasets) |

:::{admonition} Working through this on your own
:class: note
Install your chosen programs and download the datasets first — full
instructions are on the [software page](../software.md). The tutorial texts
walk through every step, from raw diffraction data to a refined structure.
:::

The whole point of this block is to work through the programs yourself and
find out where your weak spots are. The only way to learn crystallography
is, in the end, to do it.
