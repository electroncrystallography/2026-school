## What the school covered

- **[Day 1](./agenda.md#saturday-day1)** — the physics of electron diffraction, 3D ED data collection schemes, and data reduction from frames through to extracted intensities, with demonstrations of PETS2 and DIALS.
- **[Day 2](./agenda.md#sunday-day2)** — 4D-STEM, structure solution by direct methods, dual-space iterative methods, and global optimization, kinematical refinement, and a hands-on practical session.
- **[Day 3](./agenda.md#monday-day3)** — dynamical theory and dynamical refinement, serial electron crystallography, sponsor talks, and closing discussion.

## Working through the material yourself

The [practical session](./day2/practical-session.md) is built around two software tracks that you can still work through on your own:

- **PETS2 + Jana2020** for small-molecule and inorganic crystals
- **DIALS + Phenix** for macromolecular compounds

The [software page](./software.md) has installation instructions for both, along with the tutorial datasets — the Jana cookbook examples and the Zenodo archives used during the school.
